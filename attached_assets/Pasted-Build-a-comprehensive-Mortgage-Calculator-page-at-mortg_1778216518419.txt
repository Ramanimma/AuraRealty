Build a comprehensive Mortgage Calculator page at /mortgage-calculator. Do the following:
1. Create src/pages/MortgageCalculator.tsx with:
Inputs:

Purchase price (default $500,000)
Down payment amount + percentage (synced, with quick pick buttons: 3%, 5%, 10%, 20%)
Loan term select (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 years)
Interest rate input (default 7.5%)
Credit score range select (760-850, 720-759, 680-719, 640-679, 600-639, Below 600)
Property tax rate input as % (default 2%)
Home insurance rate input as % (default 0.5%)
HOA monthly fee input (default $0)
Loan type tabs: Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA

Calculations:

Standard amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1]
PMI: required if conventional loan and down payment less than 20%. Rates by credit score: 760+=0.2%, 720-759=0.4%, 680-719=0.6%, 640-679=0.9%, 600-639=1.2%, below 600=1.5% of loan amount annually
FHA: 1.75% upfront MIP added to loan + 0.85% annual MIP monthly
VA: 2.15% funding fee added to loan (first use, 0% down), no PMI
USDA: 1% upfront guarantee fee + 0.35% annual fee, no down payment required
Property tax monthly = (purchase price × tax rate) / 12
Insurance monthly = (purchase price × insurance rate) / 12

Display:

Total monthly payment prominently at top
Breakdown table: Principal & Interest, Property Tax, Home Insurance, PMI/MIP (if applicable), HOA, Total
Loan summary: Purchase price, down payment, loan amount, total of all payments, total interest paid
Income benchmarks table: show required annual income at 25%, 28%, 36%, 43% housing ratios
All values update in real time as inputs change
